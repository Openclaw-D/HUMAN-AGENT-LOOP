# STARS project instructions

## Product authority

- Treat the three-party financing-governance definition as authoritative.
- The three responsibility units are: leader + leader agent, business staff + business agent, and risk staff + risk agent.
- Preserve this sequence: leader sets direction, leader agent delegates, business executes, risk reviews, risk makes the final risk decision.
- Risk can approve, conditionally approve, or reject, and has veto authority. The leader cannot override a risk rejection.
- Do not restore or extend the former five-Agent dashboard, peer voting, or free group-chat model.

## First-version goal

- Validate authority, initiation rights, evidence ownership, challenge loops, human intervention, auditability, and reconsideration triggers.
- Reuse BPMN, DMN, Three Lines, supervisor, and A2A concepts where useful; do not claim these foundations as original algorithms.
- Do not implement full A2A protocol merely for naming. The organization-governance mechanism is the product focus.

## Workflow

- Discuss positioning and information hierarchy first.
- Produce a low-fidelity wireframe before high-fidelity visual work.
- Do not change frontend code until the user approves the wireframe or explicitly requests implementation.
- Keep exploratory replies concise unless the user asks for expansion.

## Validation

- Run `npm run lint`, `npm test`, and `npm run build` for implementation changes.
- Preserve original specialist opinions, evidence, decision records, and human approval boundaries.
